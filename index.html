Reading Comprehension

Read the two passages below and answer the corresponding comprehension questions.

Level A2

Passage 1: A Day in the Life of a National Park Ranger

Mark is a park ranger at a beautiful national park. He lives in a small cabin inside the forest. Every day, he wakes up very early, usually at 5:30 AM. First, he makes a cup of coffee and checks the weather report on his computer. Then, he starts his daily patrol.

Mark loves his job because he likes being in nature. Yesterday, he walked over 15 kilometers. During his patrol, he helped a family of tourists who were lost. He showed them a map and gave them directions back to the parking lot. Later in the afternoon, he saw a mother bear and her cubs crossing the river. He stayed far away and took photos. Mark says, "Next week, I am going to teach a special class about forest safety for children. I think we should all protect the forest and keep it clean."

Questions for Passage 1:

R1. True or False: Mark lives in a house in a nearby town.
R2. True or False: Mark helped some tourists find their way yesterday.
R3. True or False: Next week, Mark is going to teach forest safety to children.
R4. Why does Mark love his job as a national park ranger?
Sample Answer: He loves his job because he likes being in nature.
R5. What did Mark do when he saw the mother bear and her cubs?
Sample Answer: He stayed far away and took photos.
Level A2+

Passage 2: The Future of Eco-Tourism

Travelling is easier and cheaper than it was in the past. Consequently, millions of people travel to remote and exotic locations every year. However, this high volume of tourists can cause severe damage to fragile ecosystems and local communities. This is why eco-tourism is becoming more popular than traditional holidays.

Eco-tourism is a way of travelling that respects the environment. In an eco-tourist resort, guests must conserve water and should not waste electricity. Most eco-resorts are built with natural materials and use solar energy. Next summer, Lisa is going to visit an eco-lodge in Costa Rica. "I want to have a great vacation, but I also must make sure I don't hurt the rainforest," she explains. Experts believe that eco-tourism will become the most important segment of the travel industry in the next decade. If we want to keep our planet green, we must travel responsibly.

Questions for Passage 2:

R6. True or False: Eco-tourism is less popular than traditional holidays in the text, but is growing rapidly.
R7. True or False: In eco-tourism resorts, guests can waste as much water as they want.
R8. True or False: Lisa is going to travel to an eco-resort in Costa Rica next summer.
R9. What is different about the construction and energy sources of most eco-resorts compared to standard hotels?
Sample Answer: They are built with natural materials and use solar energy.
R10. What prediction do experts make about the future of the travel industry?
Sample Answer: Experts believe that eco-tourism will become the most important segment of the travel industry.
